Music#

Enmachined — Thrash Metal#

Old school thrash metal band from Dhaka, Bangladesh, formed in 2011. Influenced by East Coast Thrash and Bay Area Thrash scene. The band refers to their music as “bone crushing thrash metal.”

Released through Salute Records (Sweden) and Hellhouse 666 Productions (Thailand).
