.. _hr-system:

==========================================
HR System with Access Control Integration
==========================================

.. grid:: 1

   .. grid-item-card:: Biometric Attendance & ZKTeco Integration
      :shadow: md

      **Stack:** Django, Golang, Python (zkteco-server)

      Developed biometric attendance systems integrated with ZKTeco access control
      devices for government office deployments. The system handles:

      - **ZKTeco device communication** (TCP/IP) for real-time attendance polling
      - **Employee onboarding & credential management**
      - **Leave / attendance tracking**
      - **Government office deployment** with role-based access
